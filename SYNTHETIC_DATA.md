# Synthetic data

Every document, dataset, name, employer, salary figure and identifier in this
repository is **fabricated**. None of it derives from a real Malaysian
individual, employer or institution.

- `data/mock_documents/` contains synthetic payslips, an EPF statement, loan statements, a low-quality payslip, a
  restaurant receipt and a prompt-injection payslip, generated by `scripts/generate_mock_documents.py`. Every image
  carries a `SPECIMEN - SYNTHETIC DATA` watermark; IC and account numbers are zero placeholders.
- `data/demo_profiles.json` holds the pre-confirmed Aina, Kumar and Mei profiles used by *Load demo profile*.
- `data/property_stock.json` holds 80 fictional Klang Valley listings.

LifeNest AI **never** ingests real credit reports or CCRIS/CTOS data. A buyer's own documents are session-scoped:
stored in a private bucket with a 24-hour lifecycle, purged on request, and never written to the database or logs.
