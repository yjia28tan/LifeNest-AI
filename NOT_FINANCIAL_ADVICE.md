# Not financial advice

LifeNest AI is a **decision-support tool**, not a financial adviser and not a
credit reporting agency. Nothing it outputs constitutes:

- An offer of credit, loan pre-approval or loan approval from any bank.
- A CCRIS or CTOS credit report or a substitute for one.
- Regulated financial, legal, tax or insurance advice under Malaysian law.

Every figure it produces is computed by a deterministic engine from the versioned parameters in
[`config/rules_my_2026_07.yaml`](config/rules_my_2026_07.yaml), and every result is stamped with that
`rules_version`. The parameters are indicative: they must be verified against Bank Negara Malaysia, KWSP and LHDN
and against an individual bank's own policies before being relied upon for a real financing decision.

Buyers should consult a licensed mortgage banker, a licensed financial planner
(under the Financial Services Act 2013 / Islamic Financial Services Act 2013),
or a licensed conveyancing lawyer before entering into a Sale and Purchase
Agreement or a housing loan.
